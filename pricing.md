# SmartScanner pricing

SmartScanner offers a free version, a subscription plan for ongoing updates, and a perpetual license for offline or air-gapped environments.

## Free

- Price: $0/month
- Limits: Unlimited targets and scans; limited vulnerability details
- Includes: Basic scanning for evaluation
- Start: [Download SmartScanner](/download)

## Pro

- Monthly price: $25/month
- Annual price: $20/month, billed annually at $240 upfront
- Includes: Full vulnerability details, ongoing updates, and cloud-connected use
- Requirement: Internet connection for license activation and use
- Purchase: [View Pro pricing](/pricing)

## Perpetual

- Price: $599 per seat, one-time payment
- Best for: Air-gapped security teams and organizations that do not want another SaaS subscription
- Includes: Full vulnerability details, lifetime license, offline and air-gapped operation, and one year of upgrades
- Upgrade renewal: $219/year, or $149 when renewed before expiry
- Purchase: [Contact sales](/contact)

Prices are listed in USD. See the [human-readable pricing page](/pricing) for current plan presentation and payment details.
