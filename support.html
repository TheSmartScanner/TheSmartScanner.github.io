Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How can I scan my website with SmartScanner? +
You just need to enter your website URL and hit the scan button. Read the guide on Scanning a target page.
How Smart is SmartScanner? +
SmartScanner is powered by advanced AI and Machine Learning, allowing it to adapt its scanning methods to each unique target. This results in highly accurate detections with fewer false positives. From identifying vulnerable pages to contextual risk analysis, SmartScanner continuously learns and evolves. For more details on how we use AI, visit our AI page.
What types of vulnerabilities does SmartScanner detect? +
SmartScanner detects vulnerabilities like SQL Injection, Cross-Site Scripting (XSS), authentication flaws, known CMS bugs, and OWASP Top 10 risks. Learn more on our vulnerabilities page.
My website is behind a login form, how can I scan it with SmartScanner? +
SmartScanner supports both HTTP Basic and HTML form authentications. Read about authenticated scans.
Can you see the targets or results of my scans? +
No, we do not access or store your scan results. See our privacy policy for more.
How can I cancel my subscription? +
To cancel your subscription, open SmartScanner and view your license details by hovering over the version number in the bottom-left corner. In the license info popup, click the "Cancel Subscription" button, which will take you to a confirmation page. After canceling, your subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing cycle. For detailed instructions, visit our subscription management guide.
