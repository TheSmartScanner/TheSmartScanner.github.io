SmartScanner vs Burp Suite — Overview

SmartScanner focuses on AI-driven vulnerability detection, zero-configuration installation, affordability, and high-speed automated scanning. It is designed for developers and DevOps teams who want fast, accurate results without security expertise.

Burp Suite is the industry standard for manual penetration testing, used by professional security researchers for in-depth application analysis.

What is SmartScanner?

SmartScanner is an AI-powered automated web vulnerability scanner offering unlimited scans, flexible licensing (subscription + perpetual), blazing-fast local scans, and full CLI automation for CI/CD pipelines.

What is Burp Suite?

Burp Suite is a professional penetration testing toolkit offering manual and semi-automated scanning, proxy interception, request tampering, and advanced research tools for security professionals.

Who Should Use Which Tool?

SmartScanner is perfect for:

  • Startups & freelancers needing affordable automated scanning
  • Bug bounty hunters looking for quick reconnaissance
  • Developers wanting fast feedback without complex tools
  • DevOps teams integrating CI/CD automated scans via CLI
  • Teams without deep penetration testing expertise

Burp Suite is ideal for:

  • Professional penetration testers
  • Security researchers performing manual deep analysis
  • Advanced users needing proxy, repeater, intruder, etc.
  • Organizations with dedicated security teams
  • Large companies with complex testing requirements

Is Burp Suite the right fit?

Burp Suite remains a sensible choice when its primary workflow matches your team. SmartScanner is a better fit when you want guided automated scanning, local operation, actionable developer reports, and predictable pricing without enterprise procurement.

Choose SmartScanner when

  • You want to start a scan with minimal configuration
  • Developers need findings they can act on quickly
  • You need local scanning and CLI automation
  • You prefer free, monthly, annual, or perpetual licensing

Keep Burp Suite when

  • Your team depends on its specialized workflow
  • You need capabilities SmartScanner does not claim to replace
  • Your existing process and integrations are already effective
  • Its operating model and expertise requirements fit your team

Migration and coexistence

There is no proprietary project database to migrate into SmartScanner. Keep your existing reports, scripts, and pipeline configuration, then validate SmartScanner against an authorized staging target. Add it alongside Burp Suite first, compare coverage and remediation effort, and change pipeline gates only after the results match your risk tolerance.
