Uninstalling SmartScanner

SmartScanner includes a built-in uninstaller, making it simple to remove the application from your system. Since SmartScanner is installed for the current user, the process is straightforward and user-specific.

Steps to Uninstall SmartScanner

To uninstall SmartScanner, follow these steps:

  1. Open the Uninstaller:
    • You can access SmartScanner’s uninstaller by navigating to the Control Panel or using the Settings app in Windows.
    • From Control Panel:
      • Open the Control Panel.
      • Click on Programs > Programs and Features.
      • Locate SmartScanner in the list of installed programs.
      • Right-click and select Uninstall.
    • From Settings (Windows 10/11):
      • Open Settings by pressing Windows + I.
      • Click on Apps > Apps & Features.
      • Scroll down to find SmartScanner in the app list.
      • Click on SmartScanner, then click Uninstall.

  2. Run SmartScanner’s Built-in Uninstaller: Once you initiate the uninstall process, SmartScanner’s own uninstaller will launch. This tool will guide you through the removal steps.

  3. Confirm the Uninstallation: When prompted, confirm that you want to remove SmartScanner by clicking Yes. The uninstallation process will begin, and SmartScanner will be removed from your system.

💡 Tip: It’s recommended to use SmartScanner’s built-in uninstaller, as it preserves your license information. This way, if you reinstall SmartScanner in the future, your license will be automatically recognized, saving you the hassle of re-entering it.

By following these steps, you can easily uninstall SmartScanner from your system without losing important data such as your license.
